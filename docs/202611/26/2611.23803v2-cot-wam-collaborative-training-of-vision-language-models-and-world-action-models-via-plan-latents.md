## 摘要
通用机器人操作既需要语义能力，也需要物理预见能力。世界动作模型（WAM）通过联合建模视觉预见与动作分布，获得对物理世界的理解与预测能力。然而，现有 WAM 仍缺乏语义能力：其在开放世界场景理解、语义推理和长时程规划方面的能力有限。尽管视觉语言模型（VLM）提供了这些能力，但将其语义先验有效融入 WAM 的预测与控制仍具挑战性。为解决这一挑战，我们提出 CoT-WAM，一个联合训练的 WAM-VLM 框架。CoT-WAM 使用可学习嵌入在 VLM 与 WAM 之间交换信息，该嵌入在训练过程中由文本和目标图像信号监督，以编码丰富的意图信息。定制的两阶段训练策略实现了联合优化，并使 VLM 与 WAM 之间更有效地协作。我们在涵盖控制性能、零样本泛化和长时程规划的六个基准上评估 CoT-WAM。CoT-WAM 在 LIBERO、CALVIN 和 RoboCasa-Kitchen 上取得 SOTA 性能，并在无需微调的情况下于 LIBERO-Plus 和 LIBERO-Pro 上展现出更强的零样本泛化能力。在 RoboCasa365 上，相较于使用和不使用外部规划器的基线，它进一步提升了长时程规划能力。真实世界实验进一步证明了我们的方法在实际部署中的有效性。代码见：https://github.com/jhz1192/CoT-WAM.git

## Abstract
General robot manipulation requires both semantic competence and physical foresight. World action models (WAMs) acquire an understanding of and predictive capability over the physical world by jointly modeling visual foresight and action distributions. However, existing WAMs still lack semantic competence: they have limited ability to perform open-world scene understanding, semantic reasoning, and long-horizon planning. Although vision-language models (VLMs) provide these capabilities, effectively integrating their semantic priors into WAM prediction and control remains challenging. To address this challenge, we propose CoT-WAM, a jointly trained WAM-VLM framework. CoT-WAM uses a learnable embedding to exchange information between the VLM and the WAM, which is supervised by textual and goal-image signals during training to encode rich intent information. A tailored two-stage training strategy enables joint optimization and more effective collaboration between the VLM and WAM. We evaluate CoT-WAM on six benchmarks covering control performance, zero-shot generalization, and long-horizon planning. CoT-WAM achieves SOTA performance on LIBERO, CALVIN, and RoboCasa-Kitchen, and demonstrates stronger zero-shot generalization on LIBERO-Plus and LIBERO-Pro without fine-tuning. On RoboCasa365, it further improves long-horizon planning over baselines with and without external planners. Real-world experiments further demonstrate the effectiveness of our method in practical deployments. Code is available at: https://github.com/jhz1192/CoT-WAM.git
