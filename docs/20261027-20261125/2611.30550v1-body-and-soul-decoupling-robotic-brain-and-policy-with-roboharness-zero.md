## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作（VLA）策略能够执行单项技能，但无法完成需要组合多项技能的任务。在我们对 Xiaomi-Robotics-1 的审查中，估计 43% 的失败涉及选择错误目标或丢失任务进度跟踪。将冻结的 VLA 置于视觉-语言模型（VLM）harness 中是一种自然的补救方案。现有 harness 可通过针对任务和策略的专门准备获得显著收益，这引出了一个问题：这些收益中有多少能迁移到新任务或新策略。理想的 harness 应（i）对新任务只需最少准备，（ii）对新策略无需重新设计，（iii）保留策略已有的独立能力。据我们所知，我们的 RoboHarness-Zero 是首个以此方式构建的 harness。其前提是：对于 GPT-6 这类 VLM，无需任务准备即可轻松选择目标并跟踪进度；而冻结的 VLA 已经能够处理连续的、接触丰富的运动。因此，冻结的 VLA 执行连续控制，而 VLM 进行零样本规划并监控执行；当进度停滞或目标漂移时，VLM 首先重述指令，仅在作为后备方案时，才在通用运动原语上执行简短生成的代码，然后将精细控制交还给 VLA。监督规则在不同策略间保持不变，仅需一个薄接口适配器以及为每个策略自动剖析得到的技能配置。在 200 个 RoboCasa365 场景中，使用 GPT-6 Astra 的 RoboHarness-Zero 将冻结的 Xiaomi-Robotics-1 的成功率从 57.5% 提升到 70.5%，并保留了其独立成功案例的 90.4%（104/115），同时挽救了 85 次失败中的 37 次。在共享的 50 个场景子集上，相同规则使另外四个冻结策略各自的成功率提高 8 到 10 个百分点，而现有的基于准备的 harness 系统往往无法迁移到新策略。

## Abstract
Vision-language-action (VLA) policies can execute individual skills but fail to complete tasks that require combining several of them. In our audit of Xiaomi-Robotics-1, an estimated 43\% of failures involve choosing the wrong target or losing track of task progress. Wrapping a frozen VLA in a vision-language model (VLM) harness is a natural remedy. Existing harnesses can gain substantially from task- and policy-specific preparation, raising the question of how much of that gain transfers to a new task or policy. An ideal harness should (i) need minimal preparation for a new task, (ii) need no redesign for a new policy, and (iii) preserve standalone capabilities the policy already has. To the best of our knowledge, our \ours{} is the first harness built this way. Its premise is that choosing targets and tracking progress are easy for a VLM such as GPT-6 without task preparation, while the frozen VLA already handles continuous, contact-rich motion. The frozen VLA therefore executes continuous control while the VLM plans zero-shot and monitors execution; when progress stalls or targets drift, the VLM first rephrases the instruction and, only as a fallback, executes short generated code over generic motion primitives before returning fine control to the VLA. The supervision rules stay fixed across policies, requiring only a thin interface adapter and an automatically profiled skill configuration per policy. On 200 RoboCasa365 scenes, \ours{} with GPT-6 Astra raises the success rate of frozen Xiaomi-Robotics-1 from \textbf{\color{blue}57.5\%} to \textbf{\color{blue}70.5\%} and preserves \textbf{\color{blue}90.4\%} of its standalone successes (104/115) while rescuing 37 of 85 failures. On a shared 50-scene subset, the same rules add 8 to 10 percentage points to each of four other frozen policies, while existing preparation-based harness systems often fail to transfer to a new policy.
